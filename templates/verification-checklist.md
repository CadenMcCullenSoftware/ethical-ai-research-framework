# Verification and Release Checklist

Record **Pass**, **Needs work**, or **Not applicable**, with evidence or a short note for each item. A completed checklist is a record of review, not a guarantee.

## Scope and traceability
- [ ] Work answers the chartered question and respects scope and non-goals.
- [ ] Material claims are labeled Fact, Estimate, Interpretation, Hypothesis, or Recommendation.
- [ ] Each material claim is traceable to a source/asset or clearly marked unverified.
- [ ] Source provenance, date, relevance, quality rationale, and limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Major decisions, alternatives, risks, and scope changes appear in the decision log.

## Evidence and reasoning
- [ ] Internal consistency, calculations, and terminology have been checked.
- [ ] Important claims have been cross-checked against independent evidence where available.
- [ ] Search or inclusion choices were reviewed for selection, confirmation, and framing bias.
- [ ] Counterarguments and plausible alternatives are addressed or recorded as unresolved.
- [ ] Assumptions, uncertainty, missing evidence, and unresolved ambiguities are visible.
- [ ] Any reduced rigor for speed is documented; affected claims were re-verified, narrowed, or transparently qualified.

## Ethics and safety
- [ ] Personal data is minimized; identifiers and sensitive inferences are avoided unless justified and authorized.
- [ ] Fairness, privacy, confidentiality, and foreseeable harms have been reviewed.
- [ ] The output respects human authority and does not misrepresent artificial intelligence as the decision-maker.
- [ ] Unsafe, privacy-invasive, harmful, or ungrounded requests have not been fulfilled; safer alternatives are considered where suitable.
- [ ] Technical safeguards or defensive capabilities are not presented as guarantees without evidence.

## Communication and release
- [ ] Structure and language suit the intended audience and accessibility needs.
- [ ] The report states what is known, unknown, and needed next.
- [ ] Methodology, source record, material decision log, and limitations are summarized or attached as appropriate.
- [ ] Final language is accurate, fair, clear, and not misleading.
- [ ] Required professional formatting and disclosures are present.
